    How the scores work

    Transparent, explainable, and tunable

    Safety index (0–100)Penalties for pH, turbidity, dissolved O₂, chlorine residual, and E. coli, using BIS IS 10500 / WHO limits. Any E. coli caps the score below 60.
    Outbreak risk (0–100)Faecal indicator load, lost chlorine residual, turbidity, population exposed, and rainfall, as an early-warning heuristic.
    Leak index (0–100)Flow against the expected day/night demand curve, plus pressure sag below baseline.

    Demo data is simulated from realistic Tamil Nadu baselines. Scores support decisions and do not replace accredited lab testing.
